Website privacy

Updated November 24, 2026

This notice covers the AcreOutlook product website and its email-request flow. The separate AcreOutlook app handles accounts, field boundaries and observations; its applicable privacy information should be reviewed before entering that information.

Browsing this site

This site does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts, embedded video players or a signup database. Fonts and example images are served with the website. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection and access information needed to serve the site. When hosted on GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Preparing a beta request

The request dialog lets you enter a name, region and main crops. Those answers are used in your browser to prepare an email draft. They are not submitted to a server, saved to a browser database or added to a mailing list by this website. Refreshing the page clears this form’s in-page state, although your browser may separately offer form autofill.

Clicking “Open email draft” passes the prepared text to your email application. You choose whether to send it. Your email provider and the recipient’s email service then handle the message. The optional copy button puts the request text on your device’s clipboard.

Email contact

Messages you choose to send to support@acreoutlook.com are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss beta participation. Sending an inquiry is not an automatic newsletter subscription. Please avoid sending passwords, financial information or precise field geometry in a beta request.

Your choices

You can browse without preparing a request. To ask about an email you sent, request its deletion, or stop beta follow-up correspondence, contact support@acreoutlook.com. This website does not provide an app-account deletion control.

Links and example data

External links open services with their own privacy practices. App screenshots show illustrative fields and readings; they do not disclose real customer information.
